## The standing destination (context — slow-moving)

OurLiberty is a **factory** that builds working software prototypes *fast and to the stranger-ship handoff bar*, because it understands both the **code** it works in and the **company/situation** it builds for — and it runs with **Larry gating decisions, not couriering them.** Output feeds three consumers — TruPath, Rocket Station, the AI-services co. — with Rocket Station's **RSDPM** as the first paid mission. (Permanent "why" + the handoff bar live in [shared/NORTH-STAR.md](../shared/NORTH-STAR.md); this doc is the *current* slice of why, which moves faster.)

## The why-now (the active arc — weeks)

**Make the factory trustworthy and self-directing.** It should know its own state, know *why* it's working on what it's working on, and *propose* what's next — so Larry directs by **gating** decisions instead of being the cable that carries insight, status, and direction between a laptop chat and the droplet team.

We build this foundation *first*, on purpose: when it holds, we can point the factory at the first real client mission (RSDPM) and have it **land clean** instead of needing Larry to hold it together by hand. The self-awareness brain (State Log → Beacon reads it → Orchestrator) plus the **code graph** and **scene graph** are that foundation — and integrating these planning docs *with* those graphs is where the real leverage is.

## Done looks like (the rewrite triggers)

When these hold for a sustained stretch, this why is satisfied and the DES gets rewritten (next arc ≈ "point it at RSDPM"):

- Larry can learn **where the whole system is** and **what needs him** — without summoning Claude — and **trusts** what he sees.
- **Beacon answers from a live picture** (no longer "ignorant of what's been going on").
- The system **proposes direction** (next steps, sequencing) and Larry mostly **ratifies or redirects** rather than originates it.

## Explicitly NOT now (to keep focus)

- **Atlas (company-understanding) beyond what RSDPM actually needs.** The company-understanding half waits; the current arc is the factory understanding *itself*.

(Other longer-horizon items — the Layer-3 self-optimizing portfolio, clients beyond Rocket Station, the human-facing visual system map — simply sit beyond this weeks-arc. Not foreclosed, just not the current why.)

## What this unlocks next

The *next* DES, once this one is reached: **point the factory at RSDPM** and prove it produces real client value with the same low-couriering footprint. That's the **purpose** behind doing self-direction now — it is not a done-marker of *this* arc.
